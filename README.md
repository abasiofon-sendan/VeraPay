# VeraPay

**A unified financial layer connecting banks, wallets, and payment rails across Nigeria into one seamless system.**

---

## The Problem

Nigeria has advanced financial infrastructure, yet it remains highly fragmented. 

Consider Musa at a store: his Opay balance is short, but the money across his three other bank accounts is enough. To complete his purchase, he must open multiple apps, transfer funds back and forth between accounts, and wait for each transfer to clear before he can finally pay. This daily juggling wastes time, risks failed transfers, and creates unnecessary stress for millions of users.

---

## Our Solution

**VeraPay** is a unified financial layer that connects different banks, wallets, and payment rails into one seamless system. **It does not replace banks—it bridges them.**

Our **Smart Funding Engine** uses account aggregation and direct-debit APIs so users can instantly pull money from any linked account into a single central pool. From this pool, users can:
- Make instant payments
- Execute seamless transfers
- Manage their total funds in one place

By aggregating balances and payment capability into a single interface, VeraPay eliminates the need to jump between multiple banking apps.

---

## Who Is It For?

VeraPay is built for **everyday Nigerians**—salaried workers, freelancers, students, and small business owners—who hold accounts across two or more banks or wallets (such as Opay, PalmPay, and traditional commercial banks) and want simple, instant control of their total money without abandoning their existing banks.

---

## Key Capabilities

- **Account Aggregation:** View and manage balances across multiple traditional banks and mobile wallets in real time.
- **Smart Funding Engine:** Instantly pull funds from any linked account into a single central pool via direct-debit APIs.
- **Unified Payments & Transfers:** Pay merchants or transfer funds directly from your central pool without switching apps or waiting on intermediary bank-to-bank transfers.
